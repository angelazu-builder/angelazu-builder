# Angela Zu

Physics student and Laidlaw Scholar at the University of Oxford.

My recent work has focused on two things: using small language models to study training behaviour, and building data tools for applied research.

## Current work

### [Context-order experiments with nanoGPT](https://github.com/angelazu-builder/nanoGPT)

A small, inspectable GPT implementation and a three-iteration study of how context-length order affects training.

The first experiment produced a large result. Later checks exposed measurement problems and a confound between training order and the final context seen by the model. I reran the study with paired seeds, fixed evaluation targets and a common long-context recovery phase.

The large deficit reversed under the controlled recovery condition. A smaller ordering effect remains unresolved.

[Repository](https://github.com/angelazu-builder/nanoGPT) · [Concise technical report](https://github.com/angelazu-builder/nanoGPT/blob/main/training_dynamics_research/recovery_study/TECHNICAL_REPORT_CONCISE_final.pdf) · [Preregistration](https://github.com/angelazu-builder/nanoGPT/blob/main/training_dynamics_research/recovery_study/PREREGISTRATION.md)

### [Oxfordshire social-economy data research](https://github.com/angelazu-builder/osep-quant-ai-social-impact)

Work produced during the Laidlaw Scholars Programme with the Oxfordshire Social Economy Partnership.

The repository brings together a 3,096-entity database, public-data enrichment scripts, missing-data analysis and interactive maps. It also contains the handover reports and the assumptions and limitations behind the analysis.

[Repository](https://github.com/angelazu-builder/osep-quant-ai-social-impact) · [Interactive map](https://angelazu-builder.github.io/oxfordshire-population-map/)
